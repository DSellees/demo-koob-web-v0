## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM assessment: Not generic AI slop aesthetically — the flat black/white/beige system is a real point of view, and the Hero testimonial video is a rare, on-brand asset. But two brand.md bans confirmed: the uppercase tracked eyebrow label repeats identically above all 4 rendered sections (Hero, About, Services, ContactCTA), and About's 4 pillar cards are visually identical (icon square + heading + text).

Deterministic scan: static CLI scan (detect.mjs) came back clean (0 findings across 3 runs). Live browser overlay found 18 anti-patterns invisible to static analysis. Both scans agree: detector independently flagged the same #DCCEC2-on-light-background elements measured by hand (1.4:1, 1.5:1), corroborated identical-card-grid (icon-tile-stack) and eyebrow-label (hero-eyebrow-chip) findings. Detector also caught a heading-hierarchy skip (h2 -> h4, no h3) neither human pass looked for.

False positive: overused-font/single-font flag contradicts CLAUDE.md's explicit "Inter only" policy — intentional identity, not actionable.

## Overall Impression

The visual system is genuinely considered and disciplined. But the site currently cannot do the one thing it exists to do: a global click-blocker in App.tsx silently no-ops every link and button except the floating WhatsApp widget, and the contact form has no backend wired up. Layered under that: the brand's own accent color is illegible on light backgrounds, and mobile visibly breaks (overlapping Hero text) while hiding the page's best asset (testimonial video) below lg.

## What's Working

1. The Hero testimonial video — a real executive, a quote that directly rebuts the core skepticism PRODUCT.md names. Rare and on-brand.
2. Faithful execution of the documented design system — radius-0 with no exceptions found, flat-by-rest with shadow only on interaction.
3. Disciplined copy — no "sinergias," no "disruptivo," no numbers-as-argument anywhere reviewed.

## Priority Issues

[P0] Every interactive element on the live site is non-functional.
- Why it matters: A capture-phase click listener in src/App.tsx:51-62 calls preventDefault()/stopPropagation() on any click inside a/button, except data-whatsapp elements. Verified live: nav, hamburger, both Hero CTAs, and the contact form's submit button all do nothing.
- Fix: Remove the blocker useEffect from App.tsx, or gate it behind an explicit env flag if intentional — confirm it's stripped before real deployment.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P0] The brand accent (koob-gold #DCCEC2) fails WCAG AA on every light background it's used against — and it's the section-identifier label in 3+ sections.
- Why it matters: Measured (and corroborated by the live detector): 1.43:1 on beige, 1.54:1 on white — both need 4.5:1. PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md both mandate AA compliance.
- Fix: Derive a text-safe darker variant of the same hue for on-light use, or switch these labels to black/graphite text with a gold accent detail instead of gold text.
- Suggested command: /impeccable colorize, then /impeccable harden

[P1] Hero's 3-pillar strip overlaps illegibly on mobile (<=500px).
- Why it matters: grid-cols-3 inside a 63%-width clipped bar never collapses; at 375px, headings/descriptions visibly collide, directly under the primary CTA.
- Fix: Collapse to grid-cols-1 below lg, or move the strip out of the black bar and stack it full-width on mobile.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

[P1] The Hero video and social-proof rail are hidden below lg (hidden lg:flex) — tablet/mobile visitors never see the page's strongest trust asset.
- Why it matters: This asset most directly embodies PRODUCT.md's own success criterion ("estos han vivido la empresa desde dentro"). Cutting it for tablet/mobile removes exactly what the skeptical-director persona needs to see.
- Fix: Reflow the video to appear full-width above/below the text block on mobile/tablet instead of disappearing.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

[P2] Framer Motion entrances ignore prefers-reduced-motion.
- Why it matters: CSS media query zeroes transitions, but animations run through Framer Motion's JS engine, untouched. PRODUCT.md states this support as a hard requirement.
- Fix: Add a shared useReducedMotion() check in src/lib/animations.ts and zero durations when requested.
- Suggested command: /impeccable animate
